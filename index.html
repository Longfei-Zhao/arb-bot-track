    What this is

    Best-of-N esports series on Polymarket are priced across several related markets at once — series winner, per-map winners, exact-score outcomes. Those prices are bound by exact no-arbitrage identities. The bot maintains its own fair-value estimates, detects when the identities are violated, and executes multi-leg hedged constructions, including pure locks that profit regardless of the result. Everything runs unattended on a single VPS: two exchange WebSocket pipelines, a decision engine, risk controls, settlement, and a real-time operations dashboard.
