    What this is

    Best-of-N esports series on Polymarket are priced across several related markets at once — series winner, per-map winners, exact-score outcomes. Those prices are bound by exact no-arbitrage identities. The bot maintains its own fair-value estimates, detects when the identities are violated, and executes multi-leg hedged constructions, including pure locks that profit regardless of the result. Everything runs unattended on a single VPS: two exchange WebSocket pipelines, a decision engine, risk controls, settlement, and a real-time operations dashboard.

    adapters exchange WS/HTTP clients · DNS pinning · reconnect & heartbeat discipline ↓ platform order execution · market data · fees · fill bus · on-chain settlement authority ↓ strategies the private layer — pricing, signals, sizing (plugs into everything above)
    • Layer boundaries are enforced by a test — strategy code cannot import exchange internals; the dependency direction is checked in CI on every push.
    • Settlement trusts only on-chain resolution. Game outcomes arrive from three unreliable sources (a stats feed that reports technical wins for forfeited maps, order books that pin at $0.50 on voids, and the chain). The settle layer fails unsettled, never wrong.
    • Risk is layered: per-match worst-case caps computed from live fills, a daily circuit breaker, record-only caps on outlier signals, and post-only enforcement where quoting is involved.
    • Every decision is auditable: trace-correlated logs, a deduplicating issues panel, and a per-trade post-hoc evaluation protocol that separates model edge from execution luck and settlement variance.
    • 674 automated tests run green in ~9 seconds on every push; pricing output is snapshot-locked so refactors cannot silently change trading behaviour.

    Selected findings from operating it

    market microstructure

    A price that isn't a probability

    When a map is forfeited, its market pins at $0.50 — a settlement value, not a belief. Any model that reads order books as probabilities silently ingests poison. The fix reshaped how the whole system values evidence.

    information

    Markets in an information vacuum

    In minor-league matches no data provider covers, the only informed traders are people watching the stream. We measured minutes-long windows where the finished result traded at a discount — and learned when not to trade.

    evaluation

    Expected vs. realized, honestly

    Every fire is re-marked at the next inter-game lull, on hedged units only. This separates "the model found real edge" from "the position got lucky" — and the gap between the two is tracked as a first-class number.

    Methodology. One point per calendar day (UTC), covering every settled trade the bot executed; open positions are excluded until they settle. Figures are net of all exchange fees. Milestones mark deployed changes to risk controls, execution or settlement — the strategy iterates in production, and the curve shows it honestly. Data is exported read-only from the live trading database; no manual adjustments, no backtests spliced in.

    TypeScript · Node.js · SQLite · React — single operator. Not financial advice; past performance is not indicative of future results.
